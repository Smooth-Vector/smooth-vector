What you walk away with

A clear, written view of what's worth doing.

01
Prioritised opportunities

A shortlist of the workflows where AI or automation will pay back fastest, ranked by effort vs return, with rough cost and timeline ranges for each.

How it works

How the assessment runs.

01
Kickoff

A short scoping call to confirm what we're assessing, who we'll talk to, and what success looks like. Usually under an hour.

02
Interviews & review

Structured conversations with the people doing the work, plus a review of your tooling, data flows and existing automations. Typically 1–2 weeks.

04
Walkthrough

A live session to walk you through the findings, answer questions, and agree what (if anything) happens next. The report is yours either way.

Common questions

Questions we get asked about the assessment.

What is an AI readiness assessment?

An AI readiness assessment is a structured review of your business - processes, tools, data and team - to identify where AI and automation will actually pay back, where they're a distraction, and what you'd need to put in place before building anything.

The deliverable is a written report and a prioritised shortlist of next steps, not a sales pitch.

How is this different from the free 45-minute consultation?

The free consultation gives you a quick view on whether automation makes sense for your business and the obvious places to start.

The readiness assessment is the deeper engagement: we interview multiple people, review actual data and tooling, and produce a written report with prioritised recommendations and risk flags. Most clients start with the free call to see if a full assessment is worth it.

Who should do an AI readiness assessment?

UK SMEs - typically 5 to 200 staff - that know AI and automation matter but aren't sure where to start, where to invest, or what's hype.

It's especially useful before any significant tooling spend or before bringing in a vendor: the assessment tells you which problems are worth solving so you don't pay to build the wrong thing.

How long does an AI readiness assessment take?

Typically two to three weeks of elapsed time. We run a short kickoff, then a small number of structured interviews and a tooling and data review, then produce the written report.

Your team's time commitment is usually four to six hours total across the engagement - we do the heavy lifting.

What does an AI readiness assessment cost?

Pricing is on application and depends on the size of your team and the breadth of the review. We quote a fixed price upfront, never hourly.

The free 45-minute consultation is the right place to scope it - we'll give you a written quote off the back of that call.

Will I be pushed into buying a build at the end?

No. The assessment is a standalone deliverable. Plenty of clients take the report away and either implement themselves, hand it to an internal team, or sit on it for a quarter before deciding.

We're happy to be involved in the build if you want us to be, but that's a separate decision and a separate engagement.
