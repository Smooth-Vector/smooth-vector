Common questions

Questions we get asked about training.

Who is the AI training for?

It's built for UK SME teams - typically ops, finance, marketing, customer support and operations leads who use AI tools day-to-day or want to start.

We don't assume any technical background. Most attendees are non-developers.

What tools do you cover in the training?

ChatGPT and Claude for prompting, drafting and analysis. n8n, Zapier and Make for workflow automation.

We tailor the depth on each based on what your team already uses or is about to use - if you're standardised on Zapier we won't waste your time on n8n, and vice versa.

Do you offer on-site training, or only remote?

Both. We're based in Chelmsford, Essex, and on-site sessions across Essex, Suffolk and London are easy to arrange.

For other UK locations we'll typically run remote, which works well for hands-on workshops as long as everyone has a laptop and a working account on the tools we're covering.

How long are the workshops?

We run half-day (around 3.5 hours), full-day, and multi-session formats. Half-day sessions work well for a single tool or single team. Full-day suits broader coverage or larger groups.

Multi-session is the right call when you want people to apply what they learned, come back with questions, and go deeper.

How much does the training cost?

Pricing is on application. It depends on format, group size, location, and how much tailoring goes into the materials beforehand.

We quote a fixed price upfront, not per attendee or per hour, so you know exactly what you'll pay.

Will the workshop be tailored to our business?

Yes. Generic AI training has a low hit rate - people leave entertained but don't apply anything.

Before the session we get a short brief on your tools, processes and the use cases that matter to you, and we build the worked examples around those. The point is for people to leave with at least one thing they're going to use on Monday.

Do you offer recorded or self-serve courses?

No - all our training is live, either on-site or remote.

Recorded courses are useful for general topics but the value of working with us is in the tailoring and the hands-on time, neither of which transfers well to a recording.
